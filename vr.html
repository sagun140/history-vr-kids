Butterfly·Atlas VR

Stand at a world map the size of a table. Pick a moment in the past and a moment later on, and the threads of cause and effect lift off the map in front of you. Then walk the chain, one event at a time, into the dark.

Then press Enter VR (bottom right) in a headset.

On a Meta Quest

  1. Open the Browser app in the headset.
  2. Go to this page's https:// address. WebXR needs a secure connection — a file:// copy will show the map but cannot enter VR.
  3. Tap Enter VR, then allow the room permission if asked.
  4. Point with either controller. Hand tracking works too: point and pinch.

Tested target: Quest 2, 3 and Pro in Quest Browser. Also runs in any WebXR browser, and on a plain laptop with mouse and the W A S D keys.

What to do in there

  • Point at a dot — a card appears with the year, the place and what happened. Bigger dots changed more of what came after.
  • Tap a preset on the left panel — paper to the Moon, a volcano to Frankenstein — and watch the thread draw itself across the map.
  • Tap the timeline on the near edge of the table to move the orange Then marker or the blue Now marker.
  • Walk the thread — the map fades and the chain becomes a corridor of events stretching away from you. Step through it link by link.
  • Read aloud — turn narration on and each card is spoken.

Comfort

There is no flying and no forced movement: you stand still and the world comes to you, and walking the thread snaps from station to station rather than sliding. If you feel off, look at the floor grid for a moment or press the headset's recenter.
